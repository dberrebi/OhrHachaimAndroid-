Privacy policy

This policy covers the Ohr HaChaim app for Android. The app collects no personal data.

  • There is no account, no analytics, and no advertising.
  • The app does not send your searches, reading history, favourites, or settings anywhere. They are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes them. They are not included in Google backups or in transfers to a new phone.
  • The commentary, the weekly calendar, and search are built into the app. The app does not request the internet permission and makes no network request of its own.
  • Choosing the privacy policy opens this page in the browser. That request is made by the browser, not by the app.
  • Copy puts the passage on the clipboard. Share opens the Android share sheet. Both happen only after you ask, and the app does not choose a destination.

The app shares no data with anyone.

Questions about this policy can be sent to ohrhachaimapp@gmail.com.

Last updated: 19 November 2026.
